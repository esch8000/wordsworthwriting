About

About this site

Wordsworth Writing is an independent reference site about technical writing, editing, document formatting in Microsoft Word, and the government forms that professional services firms work with.

The history of this domain

From the early 2000s through the mid-2020s, wordsworthwriting.net was the home of an Alaska-based technical writing and editing practice. Its best-known pages covered the SF330 federal form in Microsoft Word, templates for architecture/engineering/environmental (A/E/C) firms, and technical editing services, and it accumulated links and citations from directories, forums, and reference pages over two decades.

That business no longer operates at this address, and this site is not affiliated with it or its former owner. When the domain became available in 2026, the current owner registered it and stood up this reference site so that the many old links pointing here would land on genuinely useful, current material about the same subjects — rather than on a parking page or worse.

What's here

  • The SF330, explained — the form's structure and how to manage it in Word.
  • Templates in Microsoft Word — building report, proposal, and resume templates on styles.
  • Technical editing and formatting — the levels of edit, pricing, and hiring.
  • Writing tips — short notes on usage, numbers, abbreviations, and style guides.

All content on this site is newly written. Nothing from the previous site has been copied, and no endorsement by its former operator is implied. Historical versions of the old site remain viewable in the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine.
